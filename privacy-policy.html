1. Who we are

tradeflow is operated by StarTek Systems Ltd, a company registered in [England and Wales / relevant jurisdiction], company number [NUMBER], registered address [ADDRESS]. You can contact us about privacy matters at [PRIVACY EMAIL].

2. Two kinds of data, two roles

tradeflow involves two categories of personal data, and StarTek Systems plays a different role for each:

Your data, as a trader. When you sign up for tradeflow, we collect your name, email address, and business details. For this data, StarTek Systems is the data controller — we decide how and why it's processed, and this policy explains that directly to you.

Your clients' data, which you enter into the app. As a trader using tradeflow, you enter your own clients' details — names, contact information, pet details, appointment history, and payment records. For this data, you (the trader) are the data controller, and StarTek Systems acts only as a data processor, handling that data on your instructions and for the purpose of providing you the app. Our relationship with you regarding this data is governed by a separate Data Processing Agreement, not this policy. If you're a trader's client and have questions about how your personal data is used, please contact the trader directly — they control that data, not us.

3. What we collect

About you (the trader):

  • Name, email address, password (encrypted)
  • Business name, trade, address, business phone/email
  • Any data you enter about your own business setup (working hours, pricing, etc.)

Entered by you, about your clients (processed on your behalf, per the DPA):

  • Client names, phone numbers, email addresses
  • Pet details (name, breed, size), where relevant to your trade
  • Appointment history, service records, payment/invoice records

We do not access, use, or share your clients' data for any purpose beyond providing you the tradeflow service.

4. Where your data is stored

All data is stored with our infrastructure provider, Supabase, in a UK/EU data region (London). Supabase acts as a sub-processor to StarTek Systems. You can view Supabase's own Data Processing Agreement at supabase.com/legal/dpa.

5. What we use your data for

  • Providing and operating the tradeflow app
  • Authenticating your account and keeping your data secure and isolated from other traders
  • Communicating with you about your account or the service
  • Once built: processing payments via Stripe, and sending messages to your clients via WhatsApp Business, both only with your active configuration

We do not sell your data, or your clients' data, to any third party. We do not use your data for advertising.
